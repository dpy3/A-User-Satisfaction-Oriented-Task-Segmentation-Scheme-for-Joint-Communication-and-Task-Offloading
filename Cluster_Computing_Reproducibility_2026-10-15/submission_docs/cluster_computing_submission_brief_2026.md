## Final manuscript title

`Outcome-Aligned Task Offloading for DNN Inference in Heterogeneous Fog Networks`

## Positioning

This paper should be positioned as:

1. a distributed fog scheduling and resource-management study,
2. an outcome-aligned constrained optimization study with cross-family validation,
3. a bounded repeated-control study rather than a universal overload-control claim.

## Main message to preserve

- The strongest contribution is repair-aware phenotype ranking: the optimizer is scored by the feasible schedule that is actually deployed while the unrepaired genotype is retained for diversity.
- The strongest validation is the independent GA-family transfer result, supported by a factorial mechanism ablation and a leakage-free OfflineGNN internal comparator. OfflineGNN is not cited as a published method.
- The repeated-control result is deliberately bounded: M-CA-ALM helps in recoverable congestion and near the transition, but not under immediate saturation.
