# Author Completion Checklist: Paper 1

Complete and approve the following items before uploading to the Cluster
Computing submission system. Do not retain the corresponding confirmation
placeholders in the final manuscript.

1. Confirm every author name, affiliation, email address, and the corresponding
   author designation in the manuscript and submission system.
2. Add the approved CRediT contribution statement for every author.
3. Add funding agencies and grant numbers, or an author-approved no-funding
   statement.
4. Add the approved competing-interest declaration.
5. Replace the data and code availability placeholders with a real public
   repository URL or archival DOI after the release has been created.
6. Confirm that every author approves the final PDF, LaTeX source, cover
   letter, and data-release wording.
7. Upload only the current `Submission_Pack` files; do not upload historical
   templates, archived experiments, or duplicate legacy ZIP files.
