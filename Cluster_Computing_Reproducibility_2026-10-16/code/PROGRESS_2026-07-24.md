# M-CA-ALM evidence progress (2026-07-24)

## Completed manuscript evidence

- SELECTORFORMAL: 720 rows, 240 paired environments, 30 seeds at eight scales.
- ABLATIONFORMAL: 720 rows, 180 paired environments, paired 2x2 mechanism design.
- BASELINEFORMAL: 1080 rows, 180 paired environments, six algorithms.
- ALMFull reproduces SELECTORFORMAL ALM exactly on all 180 congested rows.
- Greedy, GAHybrid, and ALM in BASELINEFORMAL reproduce SELECTORFORMAL exactly.

## Confirmed static findings

- ALM minus GAHybrid satisfaction: +0.72 to +5.28 percentage points at N=120--300.
- Repair-aware ranking main effect: +0.42 to +6.06 points.
- Guard main effect and interaction: zero in the tested factorial design.
- ALM exceeds Greedy, GAHybrid, LPRelax, DPP, and MPC in paired means at all six
  congested scales; all 30 comparisons remain significant after Holm correction.
- MPC is the strongest low-runtime baseline at high load and is about 50 times faster
  than ALM at N=300, with 3.17 points lower satisfaction.
